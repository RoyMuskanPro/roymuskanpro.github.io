# Muskan Roy — Data Analyst Portfolio

Welcome to my portfolio. I’m Muskan Roy, a data analyst based in Noida, India. I help businesses turn scattered and inconsistent data into clear reports that people can use to understand performance and make decisions.

Visit the portfolio at [muskanroy.com](https://muskanroy.com).

## What you’ll find here

The website brings together my professional background, skills, and selected data projects. Each project explains:

- the business question I wanted to answer;
- how I prepared and checked the data;
- the most important findings;
- the limits and assumptions behind the analysis; and
- the Power BI report, with an interactive preview and full-size screenshots.

## Featured projects

### GenAI Operations Analytics

An analysis of almost five million AI requests, focused on usage, estimated cost, response time, and failures. The report helps show which models and types of activity have the greatest effect on cost and service performance.

[View the GenAI Operations Analytics case study](https://muskanroy.com/projects/genai-operations-analytics/)

### Bank Loan Analysis

An analysis of more than 38,000 loan applications, covering lending volume, funded amounts, repayments, loan status, and differences across borrower and loan groups.

[View the Bank Loan Analysis case study](https://muskanroy.com/projects/bank-loan-analysis/)
